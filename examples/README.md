# Examples

Put ONE anonymized, gold-standard past output here per skill, named to match:
`engagement-plan.example.md`, `workshop-plan.example.md`, `deck-qbr.example.md`, etc.

Rules:
- Fully anonymized: no real customer names, people, metrics, or identifiable details.
- Pick your best real work, not an idealized fake — the AI learns your actual
  structure, depth, and tone from these.
- One great example beats three mediocre ones.

Skills instruct the AI to match the structure and tone of the example when one
exists. Adding examples here is the single highest-leverage way to improve
output quality.
